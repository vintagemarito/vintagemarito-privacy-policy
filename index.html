Privacy Policy

Effective: 20 May 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the personal-use Pinterest application ("the App") operated by the owner of vintagemarito.etsy.com.

Scope

The App is private, used solely by its owner to manage their own Pinterest business account. It is not distributed and does not serve third parties.

Data Accessed

Via Pinterest API v5 with scopes boards:read, pins:write, user_accounts:read. Only the owner's own account data is accessed.

Use of Data

Data is used exclusively to create pins linking to the owner's Etsy listings. No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely on private infrastructure controlled by the owner. No Pinterest user data is retained in any database.

Third Parties

No data is shared with any third party other than Pinterest itself via its official API.

User Rights

The owner may revoke the App's access at any time via Pinterest account settings.

Contact

Inquiries: via vintagemarito.etsy.com.
